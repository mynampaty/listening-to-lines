<h1>Ode to Søren</h1>

I was so ready to dismiss you,<br>
Before we first met in ‘92.<br>
I felt there was impossible tension,<br>
Between your faith and my logic/reason,

Now seeing my life in reverse,<br>
With the benefit of sharpness<br>
of hindsight, it's clear<br>
that your words did steer

me when trembling with fear I froze,<br>
onto some strange paths I chose.<br>
As a matter of fact I am now at her command,<br>
Since I managed to loosen some numskull’s hand!

I also eventually understood,<br>
In your perspective of what is good,<br>
Neighborly love is the underpin,<br>
And that racism is a sin.

Either it was pure coincidence,<br>
Or perhaps an act of Providence,<br>
That I last visited on your bicentennial.<br>
It was an atmosphere most funereal,

With rain coming down hard,<br>
I walked to the churchyard.<br>
It was damp enough to cause a sickness,<br>
Maybe even bring death and darkness.

I swung by Hans as a detour.<br>
(It was just an excuse to explore.)<br>
And finally, to your tablet–a jewel,<br>
In postscript: here lies an individual.

<p>—</p>
<em>Ravi Mynampaty</em><br>  
September 2022
