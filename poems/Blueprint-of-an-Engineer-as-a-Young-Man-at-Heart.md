Even when I seem adept I’m a pretender.  
I’m a pansy-with-hair-on-his-chest wannabe.

I agonize over my decisions which were key   
to me becoming my own dream-stealer.  
I’m a charlatan adrift at sea   
even when I seem adept. I am a pretender.
