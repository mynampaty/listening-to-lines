# How many Daughters is a Single Son Worth?   

How humanity will be redeemed:  
Many deaths won’t be a statistic.  
Daughters shall be princesses.      
Is this a certainty? Yes!  
A dynastic diamond she’ll be called,     
Single-minded, determined, redoubtable.   
Son and daughter will both be preferred. Their  
Worth? One hundred million each.  

_-Ravi Mynampaty_   
September 2026
