# The Woman in White is a Hitchcockian Psychological Gothic Horror  
_Loosely translated from Mehdi Ali Khan’s original Hindi_  

Come closer to me now and hold me tight,  
We cannot say if this enchanting night  
will repeat, or if our paths will cross  
again. We may be eternally lost.       
These fleeting moments here must be ours   
to savor, they’re written in our stars.  
Consume me now completely with your gaze,  
Cherish it for all your remaining days.    
This enchanting night may be in your fate  
only once, no matter how long you wait.  
Come closer here and now let me ravish   
your senses, for then I may truly vanish    
This monsoon of yearning which now rages  
from our eyes is one for the ages.  

_-Ravi Mynampaty_  
August 2026
