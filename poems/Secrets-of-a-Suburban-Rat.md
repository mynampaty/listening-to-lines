# Secrets of a Suburban Rat
_after Sylvia Plath’s “Sow”_

Many wonder how I’ve managed to keep  
My youthful looks.  
Whatever my secrets, I’ve hid them deep,  

But now I open those mystery books,  
Just a teeny-tiny bit,  
Offering a peek into those nooks   

Of my mind and my wit.  
How can it be  
That I appear so fit  

At an age when some replace a knee?  
Maybe I struck a devilish deal   
For my soul to be harvested with glee.  

Perhaps my friend Basil painted with zeal   
My exact likeness, a portrait, which is   
Now locked away, secured by a seal.  

Or was it the threat, not at all softened by that kiss,  
When my 40th was looming in a matter of weeks,  
That I would be traded in (shattering my bliss),  

For two 20-year-old sheikhs    
With the most excellent physiques.  

_-Ravi Mynampaty_  
August 2026
