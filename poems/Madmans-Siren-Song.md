# Madman’s Siren Song 
_Inspired by Irshad Kamil's original Urdu-Hindi_

I am your last and greatest love.  
One day when I have vanished    
You’ll find love to be elusive,  
A love that’s just beyond the horizon.  

One day I’ll have vanished,  
But I am your very shadow.  
I’m your love, just past the horizon,  
But I am in the air you breathe.  

I am your shadow eternal,  
I walk every path with you,  
In rarified air, I’m your very breath,  
I am in your every footprint.  

I walk all paths with you.  
I’m absent, but how does that matter?  
I am in your every footprint and   
My love remains your possession.  

I’m absent, but little does it matter,  
You’ll be the same, won’t you?  
My love remains in your possession,  
Even as your heart endures a void.  

Will you be the same, even then?  
When the mirror reflects you all alone,  
Will your heart endure that void?  
Or will you see me as in a mirage?  

When the mirror reflects you all alone,  
You’ll see I was unique and resplendent.  
I was dazzling--that was no mirage.  
You’ll wish I were tied to the mast by you.  

You know I’m unique, I’m resplendent.  
So let’s weave the final verse of our epic,  
because I am lashed to your mast right now.  
This epic of you and me, it’s spellbinding,  

After its final verse is woven into the fabric,  
It’ll be a song of all we want, all we sacrificed.  
Our epic, which is so spellbinding,  
You’ll realize it as we chase the escaping sun.  

It’s a song of all we want, all we possess.  
They’ll wonder if it was reality, a true story.  
They’ll hear we chased the escaping sun  
and wonder if it was only a dream.  

In reality, it was epic, and a true story.  
How will you find me again? I am elusive.  
Our story is epic, a wonder. Like in a dream    
I remain your last and greatest love.  


_by Ravi Mynampaty_  
August 2026
