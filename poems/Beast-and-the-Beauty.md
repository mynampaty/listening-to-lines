<h1>Beast, and the Beauty</h1>

Restless, confused, and uncontrollable,<br>
Your mind is a monkey swinging through trees<br>
Of thought, grabs one branch and simply lets go,<br>
only to seize another. Sounds playful.<br>
But thought-branches are your concerns and fears<br>
Life's everyday pressures and what ifs.<br>
What if you lose your job, love, or vigor?<br>
Greatest fears, and minor ones: the entire<br>
spectrum of your irrationality<br>
occupies, irritates, and exhausts.<br>
Indeed, the monkey is a mighty beast!<br>

Even when drowning in marvelous times,<br>
A monkey in the wild, un-reined, untamed,<br>
is a ball-n-chain of trepidation, dread,<br>
and hopes unrealized. Tame it and you'll see<br>
how you'll enjoy both wealth and poverty.<br>
Neither sickness nor health will affect your<br>
tranquility. Death itself can be faced<br>
with serenity. So, go tame the beast.<br>

In so doing, you will become centered,<br>
having released the demons of monkey<br>
mind. A centered being is a wonder,<br>
who's not entirely without any fear.<br>
The nature of fear is strange however;<br>
the being is most afraid of losing<br>
compassion for even his torturer.<br>

<p>—</p>
